Rights and responsibilities

Your rights: you may ask for a decision to be reviewed at any point within six weeks of receiving it. Your responsibilities: you must tell us within one month if your circumstances change.
A review is free and is carried out by someone who was not involved in the original decision. You must keep records of any payments you receive for at least two years after the claim ends.
If you disagree with the review you can appeal to an independent tribunal. Failing to report a change may mean you have to repay money you were not entitled to.
