Appeals and complaints

Do I have the right to appeal?
Yes. Everyone has the right to appeal a penalty charge notice within 28 days. We receive a large number of appeals, so please allow three weeks for a reply.
Where do I send it?
See below for our address. Appeals about green belt land go to a different team, and we will forward yours if it arrives here by mistake.
How is my appeal considered?
An officer who was not involved in the original decision looks at your case in the round before deciding.
