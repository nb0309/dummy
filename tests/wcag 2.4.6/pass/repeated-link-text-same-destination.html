Renewing an adult passport

When to renew your passport

Renew your passport if it has expired, or if it has less than six months left before you travel to some countries.

What renewing costs

A standard adult renewal costs £88.50 online, or £100 by post. The one week fast track service costs £178.

If your application is urgent, Contact us before you apply.

How long a renewal takes

Allow up to ten weeks for a standard renewal. Fast track applications are usually returned within one week of your appointment.
